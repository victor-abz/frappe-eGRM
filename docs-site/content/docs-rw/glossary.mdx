---
title: Inyandiko ziri ku byapa
description: Buri nyandiko y'Ikinyarwanda iri ku byapa by'abakozi, n'icyo isobanura mu Cyongereza.
---

Ibyapa by'abakozi muri iyi sisitemu byerekanwa mu Kinyarwanda. Iyi paji
ihuza inyandiko iri ku cyapa cyawe n'iy'Icyongereza, kugira ngo ubashe
gusobanukirwa inyandiko z'Icyongereza uhura na zo muri sisitemu cyangwa mu
zindi nyandiko.

<Callout type="info">
Amagambo ari amakuru y'umushinga aho kuba inyandiko za sisitemu — amazina
y'ibyiciro nka *Complaint*, ubwoko bw'ibibazo nka *Web Form*, n'amazina
y'amashami — aguma mu rurimi yanditswemo igihe umushinga washyirwagaho. Ni yo
mpamvu uzabona amagambo y'Icyongereza ku cyapa mu bindi byose kiri mu
Kinyarwanda.
</Callout>
