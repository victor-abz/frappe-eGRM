---
title: Kwandika ikibazo
description: Inzira ya Citizen Input y'intambwe esheshatu — icyo ubaza kuri buri ntambwe, n'icyo usubiza nyir'ikibazo ku iherezo.
---

Kanda **Collect Reports from Citizens** ku cyapa cy'itangiriro. Ibikurikiraho
ni ikiganiro cy'intambwe esheshatu. Buri ntambwe ikwereka, mu ibara ry'ivu,
amagambo yo kubwira umuntu uri imbere yawe — ushobora kuyasoma mu ijwi
riranguruye uko yanditse.

<Callout type="info">
Nta kintu cyoherezwa ahandi kugeza urangije **Intambwe ya 5**. Ushobora
kunyura mu ntambwe zose esheshatu nta murandasi na mba; ikibazo kibikwa kuri
telefoni kikoherezwa ubutaha uzaba ufite uburyo bwo guhuza.
</Callout>

## Mbere yo gutangira: hitamo umushinga

<Phone>

![Icyapa cy'ikaze, n'agasanduku ko guhitamo umushinga](../img/mobile/10-collect-welcome.png)

</Phone>

Hitamo umushinga ikibazo kirebana na wo. Niba ukorera umushinga umwe gusa,
usanzwe wahiswemo. Hanyuma ukande **NEXT**.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Twabageraho dute?

<Phone>

![Intambwe ya 1 — uburyo bwifuzwa bwo kubavugisha](../img/mobile/11-step1-contact.png)

</Phone>

Amahitamo atatu:

| Ihitamo | Rikoreshe iyo |
| --- | --- |
| **Do not receive updates** | Badashaka ko haba ikintu cyoherezwa kuri bo na gato |
| **Receive updates from facilitator** | Bazagaruka kuri wowe kugira ngo bamenye amakuru — ni ryo rifite umutekano kurusha ayandi |
| **Receive alerts directly** | Bashaka SMS cyangwa imeyili, kandi bazatanga nimero cyangwa aderesi |

Niba udashidikanya, siga kuri **Receive updates from facilitator** kandi
umenye neza ko bagenda bafite kode yabo yo gukurikirana.
</Step>

<Step>
### Ni nde uri gutanga ikibazo?

<Phone>

![Intambwe ya 2 — izina ry'umuntu cyangwa iry'itsinda](../img/mobile/12-step2-name.png)

</Phone>

Izina **ntirisabwa**. Bivuge mu ijwi riranguruye — icyapa kirabikubwira. Niba
badashaka ko amazina yabo amenyekana, siga umwanya ubusa ukomeze; ikibazo
cyandikwa kimwe.

Udusanduku dutatu turi hepfo ni ngombwa:

- **Hisha amazina** — izina ribikwa ariko ni umukozi ugiye gukemura ikibazo
  wenyine ushobora kuribona.
- **Ndi gutanga ikibazo mu izina ry'undi** — umuntu ku giti cye, cyangwa
  umuryango.

Itsinda ry'imyaka, igitsina, umwuga n'amashuri byose ntibisabwa. Bikoreshwa
mu gukora raporo y'abantu ubu buryo bugeraho koko, bityo ubyuzuze niba
nyir'ikibazo yumva ashobora kubisubiza — kandi ubisimbuke niba atabishaka.
</Step>

<Step>
### Ni iki cyabaye?

<Phone>

![Intambwe ya 3 — ibisobanuro by'ikibazo](../img/mobile/13-step3-details.png)

</Phone>

Iyi ni yo ntambwe igena niba ikibazo gishobora gukemuka.

- **Itariki** — igihe ikibazo cyabereye, atari uyu munsi. Kanda
  **Set for Today** gusa niba kiri kubaho ubu. Shyira akamenyetso kuri
  **Current event or multiple occurrences** ku kintu gikomeje.
- **Uko cyakugezeho** — In Person, Phone Call, SMS, Email, Verbal cyangwa
  Written. Hitamo inzira nyir'ikibazo yanyuzemo koko, atari igikoresho uri
  kwandikiraho.
- **Ubwoko bw'ikibazo** — Complaint, Service Issue, Question cyangwa Other.
- **Ibisobanuro** — mu magambo ya nyir'ikibazo, mu buryo burambuye uko
  ushoboye. Umugenzuzi utari aho ni ibi gusa azaba afite.

Ushobora gufatanya **ifoto**, guhitamo **ishusho** isanzweho, cyangwa gufata
**amajwi** ukoresheje utubuto dutatu turi hepfo y'ibisobanuro. Ibifatanyijwe
byoherezwa igihe telefoni yongeye kubona murandasi.
</Step>

<Step>
### Byabereye he?

<Phone>

![Intambwe ya 4 — guhitamo ahantu ku ikarita](../img/mobile/14-step4-location.png)

</Phone>

Hari uburyo bubiri bwo gushyiraho ahantu:

- **Get GPS Location** — bukoreshwe iyo uhagaze ahantu nyirizina.
- **Kanda ku ikarita** — bukoreshwe iyo nyir'ikibazo avuga ahandi hantu.
  Kanda, hanyuma ukurure akamenyetso ukosore.

Uko byagenda kose porogaramu yerekana ibipimo yafashe n'aho byaturutse.

Manuka hepfo maze porogaramu ikwereke **akarere** ikibazo kizandikwamo. Niba
ukorera akarere kamwe gusa, kahiswemo kandi kirabikubwira.

<Phone>

![Intambwe ya 4 ikomeje — akarere n'ibisobanuro by'ahantu](../img/mobile/15-step4-region.png)

</Phone>

Hepfo yaho, wongereho ikintu cyose cyafasha umuntu kubona aho hantu — isoko
ry'inzira, izina ry'ikigo, ikimenyetso. Izina ry'akarere ryonyine akenshi
ntirihagije.

<Callout type="warn">
Akarere ni ko kagena **uwakira ikibazo**. Niba ahantu nyir'ikibazo avuga
hatari mu turere ukorera, kandika ikibazo uko biri, wandike ahantu nyirizina
mu bisobanuro, hanyuma ubibwire umuyobozi wa sisitemu nyuma.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Musomere ibyanditswe

<Phone>

![Intambwe ya 5 — incamake yo kwemeza](../img/mobile/16-step5-confirm.png)

</Phone>

Porogaramu yerekana ibyo winjije byose ku cyapa kimwe. Somera nyir'ikibazo
ibisobanuro kandi ugenzure itariki. Icyo ari cyo cyose kitari cyo — subira
inyuma ukoresheje akambi kari hejuru ibumoso ugikosore.

Hanyuma ukande **SUBMIT**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Muhe kode

<Phone>

![Intambwe ya 6 — kode y'ikibazo](../img/mobile/17-step6-code.png)

</Phone>

Porogaramu itanga kode yo gukurikirana nka `RDAP-260926-4369`.

<Callout type="warn">
Somera nyir'ikibazo iyo kode kandi umenye neza ko yayanditse mbere yuko
agenda. Niba atahisemo gutanga amakuru yo kumugeraho, iyi kode ni yo nzira
yonyine azashobora kongera kurebeshaho ikibazo cye.
</Callout>

Mubwire ko ashobora kukireba kuri **egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal** munsi ya
**Track**, cyangwa agarutse kuri wowe afite kode.

Kanda **Back to Citizen Input Home** kugira ngo wandike ikindi.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Baza mbere yo kubika

Ubwiza bw'ikibazo bugenwa n'iki kiganiro gusa. Mbere y'Intambwe ya 5,
genzura ko ufite:

- **Ahantu nyirizina** — umudugudu, ikigo cyangwa ikimenyetso, atari akarere
  gusa.
- **Igihe nyirizina** — itariki, n'iyo yaba iri hafi gusa.
- **Icyagenze nabi ku buryo busobanutse**, mu magambo ya nyir'ikibazo aho
  kwandika incamake nka "ikibazo cya serivisi".
- **Niba ashaka ko bamuvugisha**, na nimero niba abishaka.
