Abakozi bo ku rwego rw'ibanze akazi kabo kenshi bagakorera kure y'ameza —
mu nama y'abaturage, mu rugendo rwo gusura ahakorerwa, mu mudugudu udafite
murandasi ihamye. Porogaramu ya eGRM yo kuri telefoni ni sisitemu imwe
n'urubuga, yagenewe telefoni, kandi ikomeza gukora n'ubwo murandasi yaba
itariho.

<Callout type="info">
Porogaramu igenewe **abakozi**, ntigenewe abaturage. Abaturage batanga
ibibazo ku rubuga rwa
**[egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal](https://egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal)** — reba
[Ku baturage](/rw/docs/citizen). Ukeneye konti y'umukozi kugira ngo urenge
icyapa cyo kwinjira.
</Callout>

## Ibyo ushobora gukora na yo

- **Kwandika ikibazo** umuturage akuzaniye, unyuze mu kiganiro cy'intambwe
  esheshatu cyanditswe kugira ngo gisomerwe mu ijwi riranguruye.
- **Kureba ibibazo waherewe**, n'intambwe buri kimwe kigezemo.
- **Kureba imibare** y'aho ukorera — ibibazo bingahe, ibyakemutse bingahe,
  n'igihe bimara.

Kugena uwakemura ikibazo no gukemura birashoboka na byo kuri porogaramu,
ariko biroroshye ku rubuga. Iyi mfashanyigisho ivuga ku kwandika no kureba.

## Fungura porogaramu

Icyapa cya mbere kibaza niba warigeze gukoresha porogaramu, kandi kikaguha
guhitamo ururimi.

<Phone>

![Icyapa cya mbere, kibaza niba warigeze gukoresha porogaramu](../img/mobile/00-welcome.png)

</Phone>

Hitamo **YES** niba usanzwe ufite konti — ni ko biri, niba umuyobozi wa
sisitemu yaragushyiriyeho konti. Ururimi uhisemo aha rukoreshwa muri
porogaramu yose, kandi ushobora kuruhindura nyuma uhereye kuri **Profile**.

## Injira

<Phone>

![Icyapa cyo kwinjira](../img/mobile/01-login.png)

</Phone>

Andika **aderesi y'imeyili n'ijambobanga** bimwe ukoresha ku rubuga. Niba
utarigeze winjira ahandi na hamwe, bisabe umuyobozi wa sisitemu.

<Callout type="warn">
Porogaramu ikeneye murandasi ikora **ubwa mbere winjiye**, no mu gihe cyose
ushaka kohereza ibyo wakusanyije. Ntiyibikeneye hagati aho — reba
[Gukora nta murandasi](/rw/docs/mobile/reports#gukora-nta-murandasi).
</Callout>

## Icyapa cy'itangiriro

<Phone>

![Icyapa cy'itangiriro cya porogaramu n'amakarita yacyo ane](../img/mobile/02-dashboard.png)

</Phone>

Amakarita ane, mu bwuzuzanye uzajya uyakoreshamo:

| Ikarita | Icyo ikora |
| --- | --- |
| **Collect Reports from Citizens** | Andika ikibazo gishya — [intambwe ku yindi](/rw/docs/mobile/record) |
| **Search Reports** | Ibibazo waherewe, bishyizwe mu matsinda hakurikijwe intambwe |
| **Sync Files** | Ohereza ibyo wakusanyije kandi ukure ibishya |
| **View Reports** | Imibare rusange n'icyerekezo cy'aho ukorera |

Hasi hari amatabi atatu — **Dashboard**, **Notifications** na **Profile**.
Profile yerekana izina ryawe, imeyili yawe, uturere ukoreramo, n'ihuza rya
**Change Password**.

</Phone>
