## Ibibazo waherewe

Kanda **Search Reports** ku cyapa cy'itangiriro. Bifungura **Summary of Your
Work**.

<Phone>

![Summary of Your Work, n'amatabi ya Open, Assigned na Resolved](../img/mobile/20-my-work.png)

</Phone>

Amatabi atatu ari hejuru, buri rimwe rifite umubare:

| Itabi | Ibirimo |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Byanditswe, bitarakemuka |
| **Assigned** | Ibyoherejwe kuri wowe byose |
| **Resolved** | Byafunzwe, hamwe n'icyemezo cyanditswe |

Buri murongo werekana uko ikibazo cyaje, kode yacyo yo gukurikirana, umurongo
wa mbere w'ibisobanuro, uwagitanze, itariki, igihe kimaze, imimerere yacyo,
n'akarere.

Igishya kiri hejuru — bityo ikibazo umaze kwandika kiza imbere, kifite
**0 days ago**.

## Gufungura ikibazo kimwe

Kanda ku murongo uwo ari wo wose.

<Phone>

![Ikibazo kimwe, cyerekana itabi rya Actions](../img/mobile/21-issue-actions.png)

</Phone>

Amatabi atatu:

- **ACTIONS** — ibyo ushobora gukora bikurikira.
- **DETAILS** — ibyanditswe byose kuri cyo.
- **HISTORY** — buri ntambwe umuntu yakoze kuri cyo, hamwe n'amazina
  n'amatariki.

Ibikorwa bikurikirana ku murongo, kandi ni igikurikira cyonyine kiboneka.
Ikibazo kiherutse kugera gitanga **Accept Issue** gusa; iyo kimaze kwemerwa gitanga
**Record Steps Taken**, hanyuma **Record Resolution**, hanyuma **Rate &
Appeal**. **Escalate** kigishyikiriza umugenzuzi iyo kidashobora gukemukira ku
rwego rwawe.

<Callout type="info">
Akabuto gafite ibara ry'ivu ntikaba kangiritse — ni intambwe iza nyuma. Kora
ukurikije urutonde.
</Callout>

### Guhamagara nyir'ikibazo

Niba nyir'ikibazo yaratanze nimero ya telefoni cyangwa iya WhatsApp igihe
atanga ikibazo, akabuto ko **guhamagara** cyangwa aka **WhatsApp** kaba kari
iruhande rw'izina rye hejuru kuri iki cyapa. Kakande umugereho utagombye
kwandukura nimero n'intoki.

Ako kabuto ntikaboneka iyo nta nimero yatanzwe, kandi iyo nyir'ikibazo yasabye
ko amazina ye aba ibanga — muri icyo gihe icyapa cyerekana **Confidential**
kandi kigafunga amakuru yo kumugeraho hamwe n'amazina.

## Imibare y'aho ukorera

Kanda **View Reports** ku cyapa cy'itangiriro.

<Phone>

![Icyapa cy'imibare](../img/mobile/30-stats.png)

</Phone>

Imibare ine iri hejuru:

| Ikarita | Icyo ibara |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Issues** | Ibyo ushobora kubona byose, hamwe n'umugabane w'uku kwezi hepfo yabyo |
| **This Week** | Ibyanditswe muri iki cyumweru |
| **Resolved** | Ibyafunzwe bifite icyemezo, n'ijanisha ry'ibyakemutse |
| **Average Resolution** | Iminsi ikibazo kimara impuzandengo |

Buri karita inagereranya n'igihe cyabanje, kugira ngo urebe niba ibintu
byiyongera cyangwa bigabanuka.

Manuka hepfo urebe icyerekezo.

<Phone>

![Icyerekezo cy'ibyiciro n'ubwoko mu mezi atatu ashize](../img/mobile/31-stats-trends.png)

</Phone>

- **Category Trends** — Complaint, Service Issue, Question, Other.
- **Type Trends** — inzira ibibazo byanyuzemo: Mobile App, In Person, Email,
  Web Form, SMS, Phone Call.

Iyi ni yo mibare ikoreshwa mu kumenya inzira abaturage bakoresha koko, ni yo
mpamvu guhitamo neza inzira ku
[ntambwe ya 3](/rw/docs/mobile/record) ari ingenzi.

## Gukora nta murandasi

Porogaramu ibika kopi y'akazi kawe kuri telefoni. Ushobora kwandika ibibazo
umunsi wose nta murandasi kandi ntacyo ubura.

Kanda **Sync Files** urebe aho ibintu bigeze.

<Phone>

![Icyapa cya Sync Files](../img/mobile/40-sync.png)

</Phone>

- **Everything synchronized** ifite akadomo k'icyatsi, hamwe n'isaha yo
  guhuza amakuru bwa nyuma, bivuga ko telefoni na seriveri bihuje.
- **Check for Updates** yohereza ibitegereje kandi ikazana ibishya. Bikore
  buri gihe wongeye kubona murandasi.
- **Download all my data again** yubaka bundi bushya kopi iri kuri telefoni.
  Uyikoreshe gusa igihe ibibazo cyangwa imishinga wari witeze bibuze.

<Callout type="warn">
Ikibazo wanditse uri mu kazi **nta wundi muntu ukibona** kugeza telefoni
ihuje amakuru. Mbere yo kurangiza umunsi, fungura **Sync Files** hanyuma
ugenzure ko handitse *Everything synchronized*.
</Callout>

### Niba guhuza amakuru byanze

Ushobora kubona *Background sync failed* mu gihe uri ahatari murandasi. Ibyo
byonyine si ikibazo — nta cyo ubura. Subira aho murandasi iboneka, ufungure
**Sync Files**, hanyuma ukande **Check for Updates**.

Niba bikomeje kunanirana murandasi ihari, sohoka uhereye kuri **Profile**
hanyuma wongere winjire. Niba n'ibyo bitagikemuye, bimenyeshe umuyobozi wa
sisitemu umubwira isaha byabereyeho.
