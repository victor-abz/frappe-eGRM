---
title: Ohereza ikibazo
description: Ifishi y'intambwe enye iri ku rubuga rusange. Ushobora kuyohereza utatanze amazina yawe.
---

Fungura **[egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal](https://egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal)**
hanyuma ukande **Submit a Complaint → Start form**, cyangwa **Submit** iri muri
menu yo hejuru.

Ifishi ifite intambwe enye. Umurongo w'utudomo uri hejuru werekana aho ugeze,
kandi **Back** buri gihe igusubiza ku ntambwe ibanza utabuze ibyo wanditse.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Vuga ubwoko bw'ikibazo

![Intambwe ya 1 — guhitamo icyiciro n'uburyo ikibazo kitugeraho](../img/submit-step1.png)

Amahitamo abiri kuri iki cyapa:

**Category** (icyiciro) — icyo ikibazo cyawe kivuga:

| Ihitamo | Ukoreshe iyo |
| --- | --- |
| **Complaint** | Hari ikitagenze neza kandi ushaka ko gikosorwa |
| **Service Issue** | Serivisi itagenda, itinze, cyangwa itaboneka |
| **Question** | Ushaka amakuru, atari igikosorwa |
| **Other** | Nta na kimwe mu byavuzwe haruguru gihuye |

**Issue Type** (ubwoko) — uburyo uri kugitanga. Iyo ugitangiye ku rubuga,
hitamo **Web Form**. Andi mahitamo (In Person, Phone Call, SMS, Email, Verbal,
Written, Mobile App) akoreshwa n'abakozi bandika ikibazo mu izina ry'undi
muntu.

**Next** iguma ifite ibara ry'ivu kugeza wahisemo rimwe muri buri kiciro.
</Step>

<Step>
### Vuga aho byabereye

![Intambwe ya 2 — guhitamo ahantu](../img/submit-step2.png)

Hitamo agace ikibazo kirebana na ko, utangirira ku rwego rwo hejuru ugana
hasi. Buri hitamo rigaragaza urwego rukurikira, bityo komeza guhitamo kugeza
nta rutonde rusigaye.

Ari byo bigena ibiro bizakira ikibazo cyawe, bityo hitamo ahantu ikibazo
cyabereye koko — atari aho utuye, niba bitandukanye.
</Step>

<Step>
### Sobanura ibyabaye

![Intambwe ya 3 — itariki n'ibisobanuro](../img/submit-step3.png)

Ibice bibiri bisabwa:

- **When did this happen?** — itariki ikibazo cyabereyeho. Itariki y'uyu munsi
  isanzwe yanditsemo; uyihindure niba ikibazo cyarabaye mbere.
- **Description** — ibyabaye, aho byabereye, n'uko byakugizeho ingaruka.
  Nibura inyuguti 10, kugeza ku 5.000.

<Callout type="warn">
Andika ibisobanuro mu magambo yawe bwite kandi ushyiremo ibintu byose byafasha
umuntu kubona ikibazo: izina ry'ahantu, itariki, nimero y'ibaruwa. Umukozi
utigeze ajya aho byabereye ni we uzabisoma.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Hitamo niba utanga aho bakugeraho

![Intambwe ya 4 — kutamenyekana cyangwa gutanga aho bakugeraho](../img/submit-step4.png)

| Ihitamo | Icyo bivuze |
| --- | --- |
| **Stay anonymous** | Ikibazo cyawe gikemurwa nta makuru yo kukugeraho. Nta muntu ushobora kuguhamagara ngo akubaze byinshi, kandi ntuzamenyeshwa igihe kizakemuka. |
| **I want updates** | Utanga nimero ya telefoni cyangwa imeyili, bityo bashobora kukugezaho aho bigeze n'icyemezo cyafashwe. |

**Stay anonymous** ni yo isanzwe ihiswemo. Uko byagenda kose uhabwa kode yo
gukurikirana, kandi ushobora ubwawe kureba aho kigeze igihe icyo ari cyo cyose.

Kanda **Submit Complaint** kugira ngo urangize.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Nyuma yo kohereza

Werekwa **kode yo gukurikirana** isa na `RDAP-260907-6503`.

<Callout type="warn">
Andika iyi kode cyangwa ufate ifoto y'icyapa mbere yo gufunga ipaji. Niba
warahisemo kutamenyekana, iyi kode ni yo **nzira yonyine** yo kureba ikibazo
cyawe nyuma — ntishobora kongera kuboneka cyangwa kukoherezwa ukundi.
</Callout>

Hanyuma reba [Kureba aho ikibazo kigeze](/rw/docs/citizen/track).

## Niba wabuze aho uhera

- **Akabuto ka Next kaguma gafite ibara ry'ivu.** Hari icyanzu gisabwa kuri
  iyo ntambwe kitarujujwe. Ku ntambwe ya 1 usabwa icyiciro n'ubwoko
  bw'ikibazo; ku ntambwe ya 3 ibisobanuro bigomba kuba bifite nibura inyuguti
  10.
- **Ahantu wari ushaka ntihari ku rutonde.** Hitamo agace kegereye kari
  haruguru yaho, hanyuma wandike ahantu nyirizina mu bisobanuro.
- **Wafunze ipaji utararangiza.** Nta kintu kibikwa kugeza ukanze
  **Submit Complaint**, bityo ugomba gutangira bundi bushya.
