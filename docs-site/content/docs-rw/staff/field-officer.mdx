Ibibazo byinshi ntibinyura ku rubuga. Umuntu aguhagarika mu nama, ahamagara
ibiro, cyangwa yohereza SMS. Akazi kawe ni ukubishyira muri sisitemu uko biri,
kugira ngo bigere ku mukozi ubikwiye kandi nyir'ikibazo abashe kugikurikirana.

## Andika ikibazo

<Callout type="info">
Uri kure y'ameza y'akazi? Ikibazo kimwe gishobora kwandikirwa kuri
[porogaramu ya telefoni](/rw/docs/mobile/record), ikora nta murandasi kandi
ikohereza amakuru igihe wongeye kubona uburyo bwo guhuza.
</Callout>

Ku rutonde ruri ibumoso, kanda **Andika Ikibazo**.

![Ifishi y'ikibazo, idafite ibyanditswe](../img/lifecycle/10-create-blank-form.png)

Uzuza ifishi:

| Icyanzu (Kinyarwanda) | Icyongereza | Icyo ushyiramo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Umushinga | Project | Umushinga ikibazo kirebana na wo |
| Icyiciro | Category | Complaint, Service Issue, Question cyangwa Other |
| Ubwoko bw'Ikibazo | Issue Type | **Uburyo cyakugezeho** — In Person, Phone Call, SMS, Email, Verbal, Written |
| Itariki y'Ikibazo | Issue Date | Igihe ikibazo cyabereye |
| Itariki yo Kwakira | Intake Date | Igihe cyakugezeho |
| Ibisobanuro | Description | Ibyabaye, mu buryo burambuye uko bishoboka |
| Akarere | Region | Aho ikibazo cyabereye |

![Ifishi imwe, yuzuye](../img/lifecycle/11-create-form-filled.png)

<Callout type="warn">
**Ubwoko bw'Ikibazo** bwandika inzira ikibazo cyanyuzemo, atari uburemere
bwacyo. Niba umuntu yaraguhamagaye, hitamo **Phone Call** — si **Web Form**,
n'ubwo uri kubyandika ku ipaji ya interineti. Iyi mibare ikoreshwa mu kumenya
inzira abaturage bakoresha koko.
</Callout>

Hanyuma ubike.

![Ikibazo cyabitswe, hamwe na kode yacyo yo gukurikirana](../img/lifecycle/12-create-saved.png)

## Ha nyir'ikibazo kode ye yo gukurikirana

Kubika bitanga kode yo gukurikirana isa na `RDAP-260907-6503`.

<Callout type="warn">
Somera nyir'ikibazo iyi kode, kandi umenye neza ko yayanditse, mbere yuko
agenda cyangwa mbere yuko urangiza guhamagara. Niba atatanze amakuru yo
kumugeraho, iyi kode ni yo nzira yonyine azashobora kongera kurebeshaho
ikibazo cye.
</Callout>

Mubwire ko ashobora kukireba kuri **egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal** munsi ya
**Track**, kandi ko bizamwereka imimerere ariko ntibyereke ibisobanuro yatanze.

## Baza mbere yo kwandika

Ubwiza bw'ikibazo buterwa cyane cyane n'ibyo wafashe muri ako kanya. Mbere yo
kubika, genzura ko ufite:

- **Ahantu nyirizina.** Izina ry'umudugudu cyangwa ry'ikigo, atari akarere
  gusa. Ari byo bigena uwakira ikibazo.
- **Itariki nyirizina.** Itariki, n'iyo yaba iri hafi gusa.
- **Icyagenze nabi ku buryo busobanutse**, mu magambo ya nyir'ikibazo,
  aho kwandika incamake nka "ikibazo cya serivisi".
- **Niba ashaka ko bamuvugisha.** Niba abishaka, fata nimero ya telefoni.
  Niba adashaka ko amazina ye amenyekana, ni uburenganzira bwe — andika
  ikibazo nta mazina, kandi umubwire ko ari ko byagenze.

## Kureba ibyo wanditse

**Ibibazo Bifunguye** ruri ku rutonde rwo ibumoso rutondekanya ibibazo
bigikorwaho.

![Urutonde rw'ibibazo n'uduto tw'imimerere](../img/lifecycle/13-issue-list-status-pills.png)

Akato gafite ibara kari mu nkingi ya **Imimerere** kerekana aho buri kibazo
kigeze:

| Akato | Icyongereza | Icyo bisobanura |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gishya | New | Cyanditswe, ntikirasuzumwa |
| Birakorwa | In Progress | Cyarahawe umukozi, kiri gukorwaho |
| Byakemuwe | Resolved | Icyemezo cyafashwe cyaranditswe |

Uzabona gusa ibibazo by'uturere ugenewe.

## Niba wabuze aho uhera

- **Akarere ukeneye ntikari ku rutonde.** Hitamo urwego rwegereye ruri
  haruguru yako, hanyuma wandike ahantu nyirizina mu bisobanuro. Hanyuma
  ubwire umuyobozi wa sisitemu ko ako karere kabuze.
- **Wabitse ufite amakuru atari yo.** Fungura ikibazo ukoreshe
  **Reassign Issue** niba uwagihawe atari we ukwiye, cyangwa usabe umugenzuzi
  ikindi cyose — iyo ikibazo kimaze koherezwa, bimwe mu byanzu birafungwa
  kugira ngo inyandiko ikomeze kwizerwa.
- **Umuntu atanga ikibazo kimwe kabiri.** Kandika uko biri hanyuma uvuge kode
  yo gukurikirana ya mbere mu bisobanuro. Umugenzuzi azabihuza.
