---
title: Umugenzuzi
description: Gusuzuma ibibazo bishya, kubyohereza ku mukozi ubikwiye, kwandika icyemezo cyafashwe, no gukemura ibyitwajwe.
---

Ni wowe utuma ikibazo kireka kuba inyandiko gusa kikaba akazi umuntu yahawe.
Ibintu bibiri biterwa na we: ko ikibazo kigera ku mukozi ubikwiye, kandi ko
ibyabaye byandikwa.

## Shaka ibigusaba kwitabwaho

Ahantu hatatu ku rutonde rwo ibumoso:

| Kinyarwanda | Icyongereza | Ibirimo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ibibazo Bifunguye | Open Issues | Ibigikorwaho byose mu turere ugenewe |
| Iby'integuro Byanjye | My Assignments | Ibibazo waherewe ku giti cyawe |
| Andika Ikibazo | Record Issue | Kwandika ikibazo gishya ubwawe |

![Urutonde rw'ibibazo](../img/issue-list.png)

Urutonde rwerekana kode yo gukurikirana, akato k'imimerere, n'inomero
y'ikibazo. Udusanduku tw'ivu turi hejuru dutoranya ibiri ku rutonde — ukurikije
umushinga, icyiciro, ishami, uwagihawe, ubwoko bw'ikibazo, imimerere cyangwa
akarere. Ibibazo bikigera bifite akato ka **Gishya**.

## Fungura ikibazo

Kanda ku murongo uwo ari wo wose.

![Ikibazo gifunguye](../img/issue-detail.png)

Amatabi ari hejuru atandukanya inyandiko mu bice:

| Kinyarwanda | Icyongereza | Ibirimo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amakuru y'Ikibazo | Issue Details | Ikibazo ubwacyo — amatariki, icyiciro, ibisobanuro, akarere, imimerere |
| Amakuru y'Umuturage | Citizen Information | Amakuru yo kumugeraho, niba yaratanzwe |
| Inyandiko z'ibikorwa by'ikibazo | Issue Activity Log | Buri gikorwa cyakozwe, mu bwuzuzanye |
| Gukurikirana Ibibazo | Issue Tracking | Aho ikibazo kigeze |
| Iyinjizwa | Escalation | Amateka y'ukwitwazwa, niba cyaritwajwe |

<Callout type="warn">
**Amakuru y'Umuturage ashobora kuba ubusa, kandi ibyo ni byo byagenwe.**
Abantu bemerewe gutanga ibibazo batamenyekana. Itabi ridafite amakuru yo
kumugeraho si ikibazo cya sisitemu kandi si ikintu cyo gukurikirana — bivuga
ko nta muntu ushobora guhamagarwa kuri iki kibazo, kandi ko igisubizo kigomba
kugezwa ku baturage binyuze kuri kode yo gukurikirana.
</Callout>

## Ha ikibazo uwakigenzura

Koresha **Assign** iri mu gasanduku ko iburyo kugira ngo wongereho umukozi,
cyangwa **Reassign Issue** iri hejuru ku ipaji kugira ngo ugihe undi mukozi.

![Ikibazo kiri guhabwa umukozi](../img/lifecycle/21-reviewer-after-assign.png)

Mbere yo kugiha umukozi, genzura ibintu bibiri:

1. **Akarere ni ko?** Kohereza ikibazo bikurikiza akarere kariho. Niba atari
   ko, ikibazo kiguma ku biro bidashobora kugikoraho.
2. **Icyiciro ni cyo?** "Question" cyanditswe nka "Complaint" cyinjira mu
   nzira yo gukemura itari ikenewe.

Byombi biroroshye gukosora ubu kuruta nyuma yuko akazi gahawe undi.

## Andika icyemezo cyafashwe

Iyo umukozi wagihawe arangije, icyemezo cyafashwe cyandikwa ku kibazo maze
imimerere igahinduka.

![Ikibazo nyuma yuko icyemezo cyanditswe](../img/lifecycle/31-resolver-after-resolve.png)

<Callout type="info">
Kwandika icyemezo bihindura imimerere y'ikibazo ikaba **Byakemuwe** kandi
bikandika izina ryawe nk'uwagikemuye. Iyo mimerere ni yo abaturage babona ku
ipaji yo gukurikirana, bityo umuntu ureba kode ye ahita abimenyeshwa igihe
ubitse — nta yindi ntambwe yo kubitangaza ikenewe.
</Callout>

Andika icyemezo ku buryo cyumvikana ku muntu utari muri byo: icyabonetse,
icyakozwe, n'igihe. Iyi nyandiko ni yo nyandiko y'icyemezo cyafashwe.

## Ibyitwajwe n'ubujurire

Amakarita abiri ari ku cyapa nyamukuru areba akazi katarangiye mu buryo
busanzwe:

- **Igenzura → Ibyitwajwe** (*Supervision → Escalated*) — ibibazo byazamuwe ku
  rwego rwo hejuru, akenshi kubera ko byarenze igihe ntarengwa cyangwa
  byasabaga ububasha umukozi wabihawe atari afite.
- **Igitekerezo → Ubujurire** (*Feedback → Appeals*) — aho umuntu yagarutse
  atanyuzwe n'icyemezo cyafashwe.

Genzura byombi kenshi. Nta na kimwe kizakugeraho nk'ubutumwa niba utaragenewe
icyo kibazo ku giti cyawe.

## Ibyo ushobora kubona n'ibyo udashobora kubona

Ubona ibibazo by'uturere ugenewe, **hamwe n'uturere turi munsi yatwo**.
Umugenzuzi ugenewe intara abona uturere turi muri yo; umukozi ugenewe akarere
kamwe abona ako gusa.

Niba ikibazo wari witeze kibuze, impamvu isanzwe ni uko atari ugenewe ako
karere, si igenamiterere ry'uburenganzira — banza ubaze umuyobozi wa sisitemu.

## Niba wabuze aho uhera

- **Ikibazo kiri mu karere katari ko.** Kosora akarere, hanyuma wongere ugihe
  umukozi. Guhindura uwagihawe wenyine bisiga inzira yo kohereza itari yo
  ubutaha.
- **Nta muntu uboneka mu ishami ryagenewe ikibazo.** Kigene undi mukozi aho
  kukireka kidafite uwagihawe — ikibazo kidafite uwagihawe nta nyirakyo
  gifite kandi nta wuzagifata.
- **Ikibazo kimwe gitanzwe kabiri.** Kemura kimwe hanyuma wandike kode yo
  gukurikirana y'ikindi mu cyemezo, kugira ngo kode zombi zihe nyir'ikibazo
  igisubizo nyakuri.
