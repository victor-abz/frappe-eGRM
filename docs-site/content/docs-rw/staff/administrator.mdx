---
title: Umuyobozi w'umushinga
description: Gufungura umushinga, kwinjizamo uturere twawo, kongeramo abakozi, no guhitamo uko ibibazo byoherezwa.
---

Umushinga ni wo ukubiyemo ibindi byose — uturere twawo, ibyiciro byawo,
abakozi bawo, n'ibibazo byawo. Kuwushyiraho bikorerwa muri **Project Setup
Wizard**, inyura mu ntambwe ku murongo kandi ikabika uko igenda.

Ku cyapa nyamukuru, fungura **Imishinga → Tangiriza Umushinga Mushya**. Kugira
ngo uhindure umushinga usanzweho, wufungure hanyuma uhitemo **Edit in Wizard**.

![Gufungura umushinga usanzweho muri wizard](../img/wizard/00-project-detail-edit-button.png)

## Intambwe ya 1 — Ita umushinga izina

![Amakuru y'umushinga](../img/wizard/01-project-info.png)

Umutwe, itariki yo gutangira n'iyo kurangiriraho (ntibisabwa), n'ibiranga
igihugu — igihugu, ururimi rw'ibanze, imiterere y'imibare n'iy'amatariki,
n'isaha.

<Callout type="warn">
**Default Language** aha ishyiraho inyandiko abaturage n'abakozi bombi babona.
Uyihitemo ubigambiriye: kuyihindura nyuma bihindura icyapa kuri buri wese uri
ku mushinga icyarimwe.
</Callout>

## Intambwe ya 2 — Ubaka imiterere y'uturere

Iyi ni yo ntambwe y'ingenzi kurusha izindi mu gushyiraho umushinga, kandi ni
yo igoye guhindura nyuma.

Banza ushyireho **inzego** — ibyiciro ubutegetsi bwawe bufite, urugero nk'
Intara, Akarere, Umurenge, Akagari.

![Inzego z'ubuyobozi](../img/wizard/02-admin-levels.png)

Hanyuma winjize uturere nyirizina muri izo nzego. Dushobora kwinjizwa mu
buryo bwo kwohereza dosiye aho kudwandika kamwe ku kandi.

![Uturere turi kwinjizwa](../img/wizard/02-admin-regions-preview.png)

![Uturere nyuma yo kwinjizwa](../img/wizard/02-admin-regions-imported.png)

<Callout type="warn">
Buri kibazo cyoherezwa hakurikijwe akarere kacyo, kandi buri mukozi abona
ibibazo hakurikijwe akarere yagenewe. Niba imiterere itari yo cyangwa yuzuye,
ibibazo bizagera ku biro bitari byo kandi abakozi ntibabashe kubona akazi
kabo. Bikosore neza mbere yo kongeramo umuntu.
</Callout>

## Intambwe ya 3 kugeza ku ya 8 — Shyiraho amagambo akoreshwa

Izi ntambwe zishyiraho intonde abantu bazahitamo. Buri yose ni imbonerahamwe
yoroshye wongeramo imirongo.

| Intambwe | Icyo ishyiraho |
| --- | --- |
| User types | Ubwoko bwa konti z'abakozi umushinga ufite |
| Departments | Amashami ashobora guhabwa ibibazo |
| Categories | Ibyo ikibazo gishobora kuvuga |
| Issue types | Inzira ibibazo binyuramo bigera kuri sisitemu |
| Citizen groups | Uko abatanze ibibazo bashyirwa mu matsinda mu raporo |
| Notification templates | Imyandikire y'ubutumwa sisitemu yohereza |

Gumisha izi ntonde ngufi. Urutonde rw'ibyiciro rufite ibintu mirongo itatu
rutuma ibibazo bitondekanywa mu buryo butandukanye, kuko abakozi babiri
bazashyira ikibazo kimwe mu byiciro bitandukanye.

## Intambwe ya 9 — Ongeramo abakozi

Abakoresha bashobora kongerwamo umwe umwe, cyangwa benshi icyarimwe bavanywe
muri dosiye.

![Intambwe y'abakoresha](../img/wizard/09-users-landing.png)

Kugira ngo winjize benshi icyarimwe, ohereza dosiye, uhuze inkingi zayo
n'ibyanzu eGRM iteze, ugenzure ibyerekanwe, hanyuma wemeze.

![Kohereza dosiye y'abakoresha](../img/wizard/09-users-bulk-upload.png)

![Guhuza inkingi](../img/wizard/09-users-bulk-mapping.png)

![Kugenzura mbere yo kwinjiza](../img/wizard/09-users-bulk-preview.png)

![Kwinjiza byarangiye](../img/wizard/09-users-bulk-done.png)

<Callout type="warn">
**Konti yonyine nta cyo itanga.** Icyo kigena ibibazo umuntu ashobora kubona
ni **akarere yagenewe** — si inshingano ze. Abakozi muri iyi sisitemu akenshi
bafite inshingano zimwe, batandukanira ku karere gusa. Umukoresha mushya
utagenewe akarere yinjira neza ariko akabona sisitemu idafite kirimo.
</Callout>

Genzura ibyerekanwe mbere yo kwemeza kwinjiza benshi icyarimwe. Gukosora
ibyinjijwe nabi ni akazi kenshi cyane kurusha gusoma ibyerekanwe.

## Intambwe ya 10 kugeza ku ya 12 — Kohereza, ibihe ntarengwa n'imimerere

![Kohereza ibibazo](../img/wizard/10-issue-routing.png)

**Kohereza** bigena ishami cyangwa inshingano bizakira ikibazo, hakurikijwe
icyiciro cyacyo n'akarere kacyo.

![Amasezerano y'urwego rwa serivisi](../img/wizard/11-slas.png)

**Ibihe ntarengwa** bigena igihe buri ntambwe ishobora kumara mbere yuko
ikibazo cyitwazwa ubwacyo.

![Imimerere y'ibibazo](../img/wizard/12-issue-statuses.png)

**Imimerere** ni intambwe ikibazo kinyuramo. Zimwe zishyirwaho akamenyetso ka
*final*, bivuze ko ikibazo kirangiye.

<Callout type="warn">
Witondere imimerere ya **final**. Gushyira akamenyetso ka final ku mimerere
irenze imwe — urugero *Resolved* na *Closed* byombi — bituma iherezo ry'ikibazo
ritumvikana, kandi aho ikibazo cyakemuwe kigwa bishobora kutaba ari ho
wagambiriye. Gumisha imimerere imwe gusa ya final keretse ufite impamvu
ifatika, kandi ugenzure ibyavuyemo ukoresheje ikibazo cy'igerageza.
</Callout>

## Intambwe ya 13 — Gutangiza

![Gutangiza umushinga](../img/wizard/13-activate-landing.png)

![Kwemeza gutangiza](../img/wizard/13-activate-confirmed.png)

Gutangiza bifungurira umushinga ibibazo nyakuri.

![Gutangiza byarangiye](../img/wizard/13-activate-done.png)

## Mbere yo kubishyikiriza abandi

Genzura buri kimwe muri ibi ubwawe aho kubyiyumvisha:

- Ohereza ikibazo cy'igerageza unyuze ku rubuga rusange hanyuma wemeze ko
  kigaragarira umukozi ukwiye kukakira.
- Injira ukoresheje konti y'umukozi usanzwe hanyuma wemeze ko abona ibibazo
  akwiye kubona — kandi ko atabona ibyo adakwiye kubona.
- Genzura ko ipaji yo gukurikirana itanga igisubizo gifatika kuri kode
  y'ikibazo cy'igerageza.
- Siba ikibazo cy'igerageza mbere yuko ikoreshwa nyakuri itangira.

## Niba wabuze aho uhera

- **Umukoresha nta cyo abona nyuma yo kwinjira.** Ntaho aragenerwa gukorera.
  Iyi ni yo raporo isanzwe cyane kurusha izindi.
- **Ibibazo bigera ku biro bitari byo.** Banza ugenzure imiterere y'uturere,
  hanyuma ugenzure amategeko yo kohereza — akarere kashyizwe ahatari ho
  gasobanura byinshi muri ibi.
- **Hari akarere kabuze.** Kongere ku rwego rukwiye; ntugakomeke ku rwego
  rutari rwo ngo wihutishe, kuko ibyo buri mukozi uri haruguru yako abona
  bikurikiza urwo rwego.
