# Send a complaint (/docs/citizen/submit)











Open &#x2A;*[egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal](https://egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal)** and
click **Submit a Complaint → Start form**, or **Submit** in the top menu.

The form has four steps. A row of dots at the top shows how far along you are,
and **Back** always returns to the previous step without losing what you typed.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Say what kind of problem it is [#say-what-kind-of-problem-it-is]

        <img alt="Step 1 — choosing a category and how the complaint is reaching us" src="__img0" />

    Two choices on this screen:

    **Category** — what your complaint is about:

    | Choice            | Use it when                                          |
    | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
    | **Complaint**     | Something went wrong and you want it put right       |
    | **Service Issue** | A service is not working, is late, or is unavailable |
    | **Question**      | You want information, not a correction               |
    | **Other**         | None of the above fits                               |

    **Issue Type** — how you are reporting it. Choosing from the website, pick
    **Web Form**. The other options (In Person, Phone Call, SMS, Email, Verbal,
    Written, Mobile App) are used by officers recording a complaint on someone
    else's behalf.

    **Next** stays grey until you have picked one of each.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Say where it happened [#say-where-it-happened]

        <img alt="Step 2 — choosing the location" src="__img1" />

    Choose the area the problem relates to, starting from the top level and
    working down. Each choice you make reveals the next level, so keep selecting
    until there are no more lists to fill.

    This is what decides which office receives your complaint, so pick the place
    the problem actually happened — not where you live, if they are different.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Describe what happened [#describe-what-happened]

        <img alt="Step 3 — the date and description" src="__img2" />

    Two required fields:

    * **When did this happen?** — the date of the problem. Today's date is filled
      in already; change it if the problem happened earlier.
    * **Description** — what happened, where, and how it affected you. At least
      10 characters, up to 5,000.

    <Callout type="warn">
      Write the description in your own words and include anything that would help
      someone find the problem: a place name, a date, a reference number. An officer
      who has never been to the location will be reading this.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Choose whether to give your contact details [#choose-whether-to-give-your-contact-details]

        <img alt="Step 4 — anonymous or contactable" src="__img3" />

    | Choice             | What happens                                                                                                                                       |
    | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | **Stay anonymous** | Your complaint is handled without any contact details. Nobody can call you for more information, and you will not be notified when it is resolved. |
    | **I want updates** | You give a phone number or email, and you can be contacted about progress and the outcome.                                                         |

    **Stay anonymous** is selected by default. Either way you still get a tracking
    code, and you can still check the status yourself at any time.

    Click **Submit Complaint** to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you submit [#after-you-submit]

You are shown a **tracking code** that looks like `RDAP-260907-6503`.

<Callout type="warn">
  Write this code down or take a photograph of the screen before you close the
  page. If you chose to stay anonymous, this code is the **only** way to check
  your complaint later — it cannot be recovered or sent to you again.
</Callout>

Then see [Check a complaint](/docs/citizen/track).

## If you get stuck [#if-you-get-stuck]

* **The Next button stays grey.** A required field on that step is still
  empty. On step 1 you need both a category and an issue type; on step 3 the
  description must be at least 10 characters.
* **Your location is not in the list.** Choose the closest area above it and
  name the exact place in the description instead.
* **You closed the page mid-way.** Nothing is saved until you press
  **Submit Complaint**, so start again from the beginning.
