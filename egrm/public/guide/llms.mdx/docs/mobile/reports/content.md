# Seeing your work (/docs/mobile/reports)













## The complaints assigned to you [#the-complaints-assigned-to-you]

Tap **Search Reports** on the home screen. It opens **Summary of Your Work**.

<Phone>
    <img alt="Summary of Your Work, with the Open, Assigned and Resolved tabs" src="__img0" />
</Phone>

Three tabs across the top, with a count on each:

| Tab          | What is in it                          |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| **Open**     | Recorded, not yet resolved             |
| **Assigned** | Everything that has been routed to you |
| **Resolved** | Closed, with an outcome recorded       |

Each row shows how the complaint arrived, its tracking code, the first line
of the description, who reported it, the date, how long ago, its status, and
the region.

The newest is at the top — so a complaint you have just recorded appears
first, with **0 days ago** against it.

## Opening one complaint [#opening-one-complaint]

Tap any row.

<Phone>
    <img alt="One complaint, showing the Actions tab" src="__img1" />
</Phone>

Three tabs:

* **ACTIONS** — what you can do next.
* **DETAILS** — everything that was recorded about it.
* **HISTORY** — every step anyone has taken on it, with names and dates.

The actions run in order, and only the next one is available. A complaint
that has just arrived offers **Accept Issue** and nothing else; once accepted
it offers **Record Steps Taken**, then **Record Resolution**, then
**Rate & Appeal**. **Escalate** sends it up to a supervisor when it cannot be
settled at your level.

<Callout type="info">
  A greyed-out button is not broken — it is a step that comes later. Work down
  the list in order.
</Callout>

## The figures for your area [#the-figures-for-your-area]

Tap **View Reports** on the home screen.

<Phone>
    <img alt="The statistics dashboard" src="__img2" />
</Phone>

Four numbers at the top:

| Card                   | What it counts                                           |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Total Issues**       | Everything you can see, with this month's share below it |
| **This Week**          | Recorded in the current week                             |
| **Resolved**           | Closed with an outcome, and the resolution rate          |
| **Average Resolution** | How many days a complaint takes, on average              |

Each card also compares against the period before it, so you can see whether
the direction is up or down.

Scroll down for the trends.

<Phone>
    <img alt="Category and type trends over the last three months" src="__img3" />
</Phone>

* **Category Trends** — Complaint, Service Issue, Question, Other.
* **Type Trends** — the channel complaints arrived through: Mobile App, In
  Person, Email, Web Form, SMS, Phone Call.

These are the figures used to decide which channels the public actually rely
on, which is why getting the channel right at
[Step 3](/docs/mobile/record) matters.

## Working without signal [#working-without-signal]

The app keeps a copy of your work on the phone. You can record complaints all
day with no network and lose nothing.

Tap **Sync Files** to see where things stand.

<Phone>
    <img alt="The Sync Files screen" src="__img4" />
</Phone>

* **Everything synchronized** with a green dot, and the time of the last
  sync, means the phone and the server agree.
* **Check for Updates** sends anything waiting and pulls down anything new.
  Do this whenever you get back into signal.
* **Download all my data again** rebuilds the phone's copy from scratch. Use
  it only if complaints or projects you expect are missing.

<Callout type="warn">
  A complaint you recorded in the field is **not visible to anyone else** until
  the phone has synced. Before you finish for the day, open **Sync Files** and
  check that it says *Everything synchronized*.
</Callout>

### If sync fails [#if-sync-fails]

You may see *Background sync failed* while you are out of coverage. That on
its own is not a problem — nothing is lost. Get back into signal, open
**Sync Files**, and tap **Check for Updates**.

If it still fails with a good connection, sign out from **Profile** and sign
in again. If that does not clear it, report it to your administrator with the
time it happened.
