For the public

Send a complaint

A four-step form on the public website. You can send it without giving your name.

Open egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal and click Submit a Complaint → Start form, or Submit in the top menu.

The form has four steps. A row of dots at the top shows how far along you are, and Back always returns to the previous step without losing what you typed.

Say what kind of problem it is

Two choices on this screen:

Category — what your complaint is about:

ChoiceUse it when
ComplaintSomething went wrong and you want it put right
Service IssueA service is not working, is late, or is unavailable
QuestionYou want information, not a correction
OtherNone of the above fits

Issue Type — how you are reporting it. Choosing from the website, pick Web Form. The other options (In Person, Phone Call, SMS, Email, Verbal, Written, Mobile App) are used by officers recording a complaint on someone else's behalf.

Next stays grey until you have picked one of each.

Say where it happened

Choose the area the problem relates to, starting from the top level and working down. Each choice you make reveals the next level, so keep selecting until there are no more lists to fill.

This is what decides which office receives your complaint, so pick the place the problem actually happened — not where you live, if they are different.

Describe what happened

Two required fields:

  • When did this happen? — the date of the problem. Today's date is filled in already; change it if the problem happened earlier.
  • Description — what happened, where, and how it affected you. At least 10 characters, up to 5,000.

Write the description in your own words and include anything that would help someone find the problem: a place name, a date, a reference number. An officer who has never been to the location will be reading this.

Choose whether to give your contact details

ChoiceWhat happens
Stay anonymousYour complaint is handled without any contact details. Nobody can call you for more information, and you will not be notified when it is resolved.
I want updatesYou give a phone number or email, and you can be contacted about progress and the outcome.

Stay anonymous is selected by default. Either way you still get a tracking code, and you can still check the status yourself at any time.

Click Submit Complaint to finish.

After you submit

You are shown a tracking code that looks like RDAP-260907-6503.

Write this code down or take a photograph of the screen before you close the page. If you chose to stay anonymous, this code is the only way to check your complaint later — it cannot be recovered or sent to you again.

Then see Check a complaint.

If you get stuck

  • The Next button stays grey. A required field on that step is still empty. On step 1 you need both a category and an issue type; on step 3 the description must be at least 10 characters.
  • Your location is not in the list. Choose the closest area above it and name the exact place in the description instead.
  • You closed the page mid-way. Nothing is saved until you press Submit Complaint, so start again from the beginning.
