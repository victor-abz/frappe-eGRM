Seeing your work

The complaints assigned to you, the figures for your area, and how syncing works when you have no signal.

The complaints assigned to you

Tap Search Reports on the home screen. It opens Summary of Your Work.

Three tabs across the top, with a count on each:

TabWhat is in it
OpenRecorded, not yet resolved
AssignedEverything that has been routed to you
ResolvedClosed, with an outcome recorded

Each row shows how the complaint arrived, its tracking code, the first line of the description, who reported it, the date, how long ago, its status, and the region.

The newest is at the top — so a complaint you have just recorded appears first, with 0 days ago against it.

Opening one complaint

Tap any row.

Three tabs:

  • ACTIONS — what you can do next.
  • DETAILS — everything that was recorded about it.
  • HISTORY — every step anyone has taken on it, with names and dates.

The actions run in order, and only the next one is available. A complaint that has just arrived offers Accept Issue and nothing else; once accepted it offers Record Steps Taken, then Record Resolution, then Rate & Appeal. Escalate sends it up to a supervisor when it cannot be settled at your level.

A greyed-out button is not broken — it is a step that comes later. Work down the list in order.

Calling the person back

If the person left a phone or WhatsApp number when they reported, a call or WhatsApp button sits next to their name at the top of this screen. Tap it to reach them without copying the number out by hand.

The button is absent when no number was given, and when the person asked for their name to be kept confidential — in that case the screen shows Confidential and withholds the contact details along with the name.

The figures for your area

Tap View Reports on the home screen.

Four numbers at the top:

CardWhat it counts
Total IssuesEverything you can see, with this month's share below it
This WeekRecorded in the current week
ResolvedClosed with an outcome, and the resolution rate
Average ResolutionHow many days a complaint takes, on average

Each card also compares against the period before it, so you can see whether the direction is up or down.

Scroll down for the trends.

  • Category Trends — Complaint, Service Issue, Question, Other.
  • Type Trends — the channel complaints arrived through: Mobile App, In Person, Email, Web Form, SMS, Phone Call.

These are the figures used to decide which channels the public actually rely on, which is why getting the channel right at Step 3 matters.

Working without signal

The app keeps a copy of your work on the phone. You can record complaints all day with no network and lose nothing.

Tap Sync Files to see where things stand.

  • Everything synchronized with a green dot, and the time of the last sync, means the phone and the server agree.
  • Check for Updates sends anything waiting and pulls down anything new. Do this whenever you get back into signal.
  • Download all my data again rebuilds the phone's copy from scratch. Use it only if complaints or projects you expect are missing.

A complaint you recorded in the field is not visible to anyone else until the phone has synced. Before you finish for the day, open Sync Files and check that it says Everything synchronized.

If sync fails

You may see Background sync failed while you are out of coverage. That on its own is not a problem — nothing is lost. Get back into signal, open Sync Files, and tap Check for Updates.

If it still fails with a good connection, sign out from Profile and sign in again. If that does not clear it, report it to your administrator with the time it happened.
