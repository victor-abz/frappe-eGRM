    Uburyo bwo gukoresha eGRM

    Uko watanga ikibazo, n’uko gikemurwa. Byanditswe ku bantu bakoresha sisitemu, atari ku bayubaka.

    Nshaka gutanga ikibazoOhereza ikibazo ukoresheje urubuga rusange, hanyuma ukurikirane aho kigeze. Nta konti ukeneye.Nakira ibibazo by’abaturageAkira ikibazo imbonankubone, kuri telefoni cyangwa kuri SMS, hanyuma ukinjize muri sisitemu.Ngena uwakemura ikibazo kandi nkandika icyemezoGenzura ibibazo bishya, ubyohereze ku mukozi ubikwiye, wandike icyemezo cyafashwe.Nshyiraho kandi nyobora sisitemuFungura umushinga, winjizemo uturere, wongeremo abakozi, uhitemo uko ibibazo byoherezwa.Nkorera kuri telefoniAndika ibibazo uri mu kazi ukoresheje porogaramu ya telefoni, waba ufite murandasi cyangwa utayifite.

    Ntuzi aho gutangirira? Tangirira ku ntangiriro y’iyi mfashanyigisho.
