Porogaramu ya telefoni

Icyo porogaramu ya eGRM yo kuri telefoni ikora, uko winjiramo, n'icyo utubuto tune turi ku cyapa cy'itangiriro dukora.

Abakozi bo ku rwego rw'ibanze akazi kabo kenshi bagakorera kure y'ameza — mu nama y'abaturage, mu rugendo rwo gusura ahakorerwa, mu mudugudu udafite murandasi ihamye. Porogaramu ya eGRM yo kuri telefoni ni sisitemu imwe n'urubuga, yagenewe telefoni, kandi ikomeza gukora n'ubwo murandasi yaba itariho.

Porogaramu igenewe abakozi, ntigenewe abaturage. Abaturage batanga ibibazo ku rubuga rwa egrm.risa.gov.rw/grm-portal — reba Ku baturage. Ukeneye konti y'umukozi kugira ngo urenge icyapa cyo kwinjira.

Ibyo ushobora gukora na yo

  • Kwandika ikibazo umuturage akuzaniye, unyuze mu kiganiro cy'intambwe esheshatu cyanditswe kugira ngo gisomerwe mu ijwi riranguruye.
  • Kureba ibibazo waherewe, n'intambwe buri kimwe kigezemo.
  • Kureba imibare y'aho ukorera — ibibazo bingahe, ibyakemutse bingahe, n'igihe bimara.

Kugena uwakemura ikibazo no gukemura birashoboka na byo kuri porogaramu, ariko biroroshye ku rubuga. Iyi mfashanyigisho ivuga ku kwandika no kureba.

Fungura porogaramu

Icyapa cya mbere kibaza niba warigeze gukoresha porogaramu, kandi kikaguha guhitamo ururimi.

Hitamo YES niba usanzwe ufite konti — ni ko biri, niba umuyobozi wa sisitemu yaragushyiriyeho konti. Ururimi uhisemo aha rukoreshwa muri porogaramu yose, kandi ushobora kuruhindura nyuma uhereye kuri Profile.

Injira

Andika aderesi y'imeyili n'ijambobanga bimwe ukoresha ku rubuga. Niba utarigeze winjira ahandi na hamwe, bisabe umuyobozi wa sisitemu.

Porogaramu ikeneye murandasi ikora ubwa mbere winjiye, no mu gihe cyose ushaka kohereza ibyo wakusanyije. Ntiyibikeneye hagati aho — reba Gukora nta murandasi.

Icyapa cy'itangiriro

Amakarita ane, mu bwuzuzanye uzajya uyakoreshamo:

IkaritaIcyo ikora
Collect Reports from CitizensAndika ikibazo gishya — intambwe ku yindi
Search ReportsIbibazo waherewe, bishyizwe mu matsinda hakurikijwe intambwe
Sync FilesOhereza ibyo wakusanyije kandi ukure ibishya
View ReportsImibare rusange n'icyerekezo cy'aho ukorera

Hasi hari amatabi atatu — Dashboard, Notifications na Profile. Profile yerekana izina ryawe, imeyili yawe, uturere ukoreramo, n'ihuza rya Change Password.

Umuyobozi w'umushinga

Gufungura umushinga, kwinjizamo uturere twawo, kongeramo abakozi, no guhitamo uko ibibazo byoherezwa.

Kwandika ikibazo

Inzira ya Citizen Input y'intambwe esheshatu — icyo ubaza kuri buri ntambwe, n'icyo usubiza nyir'ikibazo ku iherezo.
