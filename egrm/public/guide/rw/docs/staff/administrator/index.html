Umuyobozi w'umushinga

Gufungura umushinga, kwinjizamo uturere twawo, kongeramo abakozi, no guhitamo uko ibibazo byoherezwa.

Umushinga ni wo ukubiyemo ibindi byose — uturere twawo, ibyiciro byawo, abakozi bawo, n'ibibazo byawo. Kuwushyiraho bikorerwa muri Project Setup Wizard, inyura mu ntambwe ku murongo kandi ikabika uko igenda.

Ku cyapa nyamukuru, fungura Imishinga → Tangiriza Umushinga Mushya. Kugira ngo uhindure umushinga usanzweho, wufungure hanyuma uhitemo Edit in Wizard.

Intambwe ya 1 — Ita umushinga izina

Umutwe, itariki yo gutangira n'iyo kurangiriraho (ntibisabwa), n'ibiranga igihugu — igihugu, ururimi rw'ibanze, imiterere y'imibare n'iy'amatariki, n'isaha.

Default Language aha ishyiraho inyandiko abaturage n'abakozi bombi babona. Uyihitemo ubigambiriye: kuyihindura nyuma bihindura icyapa kuri buri wese uri ku mushinga icyarimwe.

Intambwe ya 2 — Ubaka imiterere y'uturere

Iyi ni yo ntambwe y'ingenzi kurusha izindi mu gushyiraho umushinga, kandi ni yo igoye guhindura nyuma.

Banza ushyireho inzego — ibyiciro ubutegetsi bwawe bufite, urugero nk' Intara, Akarere, Umurenge, Akagari.

Hanyuma winjize uturere nyirizina muri izo nzego. Dushobora kwinjizwa mu buryo bwo kwohereza dosiye aho kudwandika kamwe ku kandi.

Buri kibazo cyoherezwa hakurikijwe akarere kacyo, kandi buri mukozi abona ibibazo hakurikijwe akarere yagenewe. Niba imiterere itari yo cyangwa yuzuye, ibibazo bizagera ku biro bitari byo kandi abakozi ntibabashe kubona akazi kabo. Bikosore neza mbere yo kongeramo umuntu.

Intambwe ya 9 — Ongeramo abakozi

Abakoresha bashobora kongerwamo umwe umwe, cyangwa benshi icyarimwe bavanywe muri dosiye.

Kugira ngo winjize benshi icyarimwe, ohereza dosiye, uhuze inkingi zayo n'ibyanzu eGRM iteze, ugenzure ibyerekanwe, hanyuma wemeze.

Konti yonyine nta cyo itanga. Icyo kigena ibibazo umuntu ashobora kubona ni akarere yagenewe — si inshingano ze. Abakozi muri iyi sisitemu akenshi bafite inshingano zimwe, batandukanira ku karere gusa. Umukoresha mushya utagenewe akarere yinjira neza ariko akabona sisitemu idafite kirimo.

Genzura ibyerekanwe mbere yo kwemeza kwinjiza benshi icyarimwe. Gukosora ibyinjijwe nabi ni akazi kenshi cyane kurusha gusoma ibyerekanwe.

Intambwe ya 10 kugeza ku ya 12 — Kohereza, ibihe ntarengwa n'imimerere

Kohereza bigena ishami cyangwa inshingano bizakira ikibazo, hakurikijwe icyiciro cyacyo n'akarere kacyo.

Ibihe ntarengwa bigena igihe buri ntambwe ishobora kumara mbere yuko ikibazo cyitwazwa ubwacyo.

Imimerere ni intambwe ikibazo kinyuramo. Zimwe zishyirwaho akamenyetso ka final, bivuze ko ikibazo kirangiye.

Witondere imimerere ya final. Gushyira akamenyetso ka final ku mimerere irenze imwe — urugero Resolved na Closed byombi — bituma iherezo ry'ikibazo ritumvikana, kandi aho ikibazo cyakemuwe kigwa bishobora kutaba ari ho wagambiriye. Gumisha imimerere imwe gusa ya final keretse ufite impamvu ifatika, kandi ugenzure ibyavuyemo ukoresheje ikibazo cy'igerageza.

Intambwe ya 13 — Gutangiza

Gutangiza bifungurira umushinga ibibazo nyakuri.

Mbere yo kubishyikiriza abandi

Genzura buri kimwe muri ibi ubwawe aho kubyiyumvisha:

  • Ohereza ikibazo cy'igerageza unyuze ku rubuga rusange hanyuma wemeze ko kigaragarira umukozi ukwiye kukakira.
  • Injira ukoresheje konti y'umukozi usanzwe hanyuma wemeze ko abona ibibazo akwiye kubona — kandi ko atabona ibyo adakwiye kubona.
  • Genzura ko ipaji yo gukurikirana itanga igisubizo gifatika kuri kode y'ikibazo cy'igerageza.
  • Siba ikibazo cy'igerageza mbere yuko ikoreshwa nyakuri itangira.

Niba wabuze aho uhera

  • Umukoresha nta cyo abona nyuma yo kwinjira. Ntaho aragenerwa gukorera. Iyi ni yo raporo isanzwe cyane kurusha izindi.
  • Ibibazo bigera ku biro bitari byo. Banza ugenzure imiterere y'uturere, hanyuma ugenzure amategeko yo kohereza — akarere kashyizwe ahatari ho gasobanura byinshi muri ibi.
  • Hari akarere kabuze. Kongere ku rwego rukwiye; ntugakomeke ku rwego rutari rwo ngo wihutishe, kuko ibyo buri mukozi uri haruguru yako abona bikurikiza urwo rwego.

Umugenzuzi

Gusuzuma ibibazo bishya, kubyohereza ku mukozi ubikwiye, kwandika icyemezo cyafashwe, no gukemura ibyitwajwe.

Porogaramu ya telefoni

Icyo porogaramu ya eGRM yo kuri telefoni ikora, uko winjiramo, n'icyo utubuto tune turi ku cyapa cy'itangiriro dukora.
